      How the picks are ranked

      The helper plays thousands of spins of your build on the real 3×5 grid: your symbol values and chances, luck (including the machine's own lucky spins), every pattern shape, the rule that a bigger pattern hides the smaller ones inside it, retriggers, symbol modifiers, 666s and Red Button charms.

      Then it adds each charm or phone call to your build and measures the difference: coins per round right away, and on average over the next rounds. The slider sets how far ahead it looks: all the way left means this deadline only, all the way right means about four deadlines. Charms that grow permanently (golden symbols, multipliers that climb) shine further right.

      On top of the coins it weighs tickets, charm space, 666 protection and risk, and combos with what you already own. A few effects can't be simulated (Megaphone, Vorago and friends); for those it uses this site's curated ratings.

      The more of your in-game numbers you type in, the closer the simulation is to your actual run.
